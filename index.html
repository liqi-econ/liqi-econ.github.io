ABOUT

Economics across disciplines and contexts

I am an economist whose research and teaching examine how people, institutions, and economic environments shape decision-making and development. My work spans behavioral economics and finance, East Asian and transitional economies, migration and health, gender and risk preferences, and economics education.

More recently, I have extended my training in field interventions and experimental design to study the use of artificial intelligence in economics education, with particular interest in data-driven learning and lowering technical barriers to meaningful empirical analysis.
