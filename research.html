Academic Research

My research examines how individuals, institutions, and economies respond to change. My work spans behavioral economics and finance, economic development and East Asia, and emerging questions about artificial intelligence and economics education.

01

Behavioral Economics & Finance

My work in behavioral economics and finance examines how individuals make decisions under risk and uncertainty, with particular attention to financial decision-making, gender, preferences, and behavior.
