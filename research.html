Academic Research

My research examines how individuals, institutions, and economies respond to change. My work spans behavioral economics and finance, economic development and East Asia, and emerging questions about artificial intelligence and economics education.

03

Economics Education & AI

My recent research explores how artificial intelligence is reshaping economics education and empirical learning. I am particularly interested in whether AI can lower technical barriers to data analysis, expand opportunities for students to engage in authentic economic research, and support more accessible and effective approaches to teaching economics.

Current Research & Projects

Lowering Barriers to OER and AI in Economics Education
with Kristine West and Caroline Krafft
Research-practice partnership examining how OER and AI-supported instruction can affect adoption, student engagement, confidence, persistence, and learning in economics.

AI-Supported Empirical Learning in Economics
Research examining whether generative AI can lower technical barriers that traditionally limit students' participation in data-intensive economic analysis and applied research.

AI-Assisted Assessment in Economics Education
Research on the validity and efficiency of AI-assisted evaluation of student writing and the development of hybrid human-AI assessment protocols.

Selected Presentations & Teaching Experiments

AI and Data-Driven Learning in Economics
Classroom experiments integrating generative AI with applied economic data analysis, experiential learning, and student research.

Community-Engaged Economic Data Analysis
Student research collaborations connecting economics coursework with real-world organizational data and applied consulting projects.
